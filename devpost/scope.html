CheckdIn Scope · approved

An airport networking web app (mobile and desktop) that matches travelers at the same airport, with similar boarding times and shared interests, and lets them chat to agree on a cafe or bar to meet at.

The unique kernel

Matching is built around airport time and place, not just interests. Two people match when they are at the same airport, board within an hour of each other, and share interests plus a cafe/bar or food preference. That "where, and do we have time" layer is what WhatsApp-status posts and chat groups don't offer.

Who it's for, and the core loop

Today this person sits alone at a coffee shop, bar or lounge, or reads a book. They'd like to meet someone new to open up an idea, learn about user trends, deepen an existing idea, or validate a thesis with a like-minded person.

What "working" looks like: four flows

The "oh, that's cool" beat: the ranked list appears, each person with a suggested place to meet, and tapping chat opens a conversation that gets a reply.

Now — the proof-of-concept boundary
  • The four flows, working in a browser on phone-sized and desktop screens.
  • Matching on same airport, boarding time within one hour, and interest overlap, ranked by shared interest.
  • Matched people are pre-made sample people; chat replies are simulated.
  • Phone numbers and social profiles are never collected or shown.
Later
  • Real people finding each other and chatting live across phones (needs a shared backend).
  • A mutual request-and-accept step before chat opens.
  • Meetups of three or more people.
  • Safety features such as blocking and reporting.
  • Real flight and boarding verification, and real airport and venue data.
Explicitly cut
  • Live two-phone chat between real users: a bigger build than fits a 2–4 hour proof of concept.
  • Accounts and login: not needed to prove the matching and chat.
  • Push notifications: not needed for the demo.
Identity and why it matters

Look and feel: not stated yet. 3-prd will ask. The only reference so far is people posting on WhatsApp status or chat groups to find others at their airport.

Why it matters: excitement about connecting people at the airport for leisure and networking, and a goal of learning to build a production app with a coding agent.
