CheckdIn Product requirements · approved

A mobile and desktop web app that matches travelers at the same airport, with similar boarding times and shared interests, so they can chat and agree on a place to meet.

The core journey

Scope said "the ranked list appears and tapping chat opens a conversation." Now we know exactly what is on each screen, which fields are required, and who counts as a match.

Screen by screen

Who appears in the list

Required for every match: same airport and boarding times within one hour. Then either an interest rule or a venue rule is enough.

Interest rules
  • A shared interest matches.
  • "Just open to talk" matches anyone at that airport.
  • Startups, Technology and Markets match each other.
Venue rules
  • Bar and Restaurant match each other.
  • Cafe and Lounge match each other.
  • "Any place is good" matches anyone.
Order and limit
  • Closest boarding time first, then interest match, then venue match.
  • At most five people.

Look and feel

  • Friendly type, minimal screens, LinkedIn-inspired with a single white background (ordinary text is dark).
  • Start, Continue and Start Over buttons: white text on green.
  • The departures-board picture is for the Welcome screen only.

States

  • First use: Welcome with Start disabled until airport and boarding time are filled.
  • No matches: "No matches found. Start over and try expanding your interests & choices," then a green Start Over button back to Welcome.
  • Chat memory: kept up to 3 hours in the browser session only.
  • Privacy: no phone numbers or social profiles anywhere.
Building now · Deferred · Non-goals

Building now

  • The five screens with required fields and disabled buttons.
  • Matching and ordering over pre-made sample people.
  • Chat with back arrow, preset "Hi," and 3-hour session memory.
  • The no-matches message.

Deferred

  • Real people chatting live across phones.
  • Request-and-accept before chat.
  • Accounts and login.

Non-goals

  • No phone numbers or social profiles.
  • No push notifications.

Assumptions and open questions

  • Confirmed: minutes 00–59, no automatic replies after the first "Hi," and the Start Over message and button.
  • Still assumed: boarding time means today.
  • Still assumed: the Interests screen button is the same green Continue.
  • Still assumed: ordinary text is dark on the white background.
  • Nothing blocks the spec.
